# MoneyHub Product Vision

## Summary

MoneyHub is a web SaaS platform for personal and small-business financial management. It helps users organize financial data, understand cash flow, plan goals, and receive contextual guidance from an AI financial assistant.

## Target Users

- Individuals who need recurring control over income, expenses, bills, goals, and financial commitments.
- Freelancers and small businesses that need cost centers, accounts receivable, accounts payable, and cash-flow visibility.
- Users who want plain-language financial explanations based on their own data.

## Product Principles

- Mobile-first workflows with a complete desktop experience.
- Privacy-by-design for financial and AI features.
- Clear financial language instead of accounting-heavy jargon.
- Incremental delivery through documented specs and small tasks.
- Test coverage and security are part of every feature, not a final phase.

## Initial Success Criteria

- Users can authenticate securely.
- Users can manage core financial records by category and cost center.
- Users can view dashboard KPIs and upcoming financial obligations.
- Users can ask an AI assistant for explanations and insights scoped to their own data.
