## AI

- `LLM_PROVIDER`
- `LLM_API_KEY`
- `LLM_MODEL`

## Rules

- Do not commit `.env`.
- Do not log secrets.
- Keep `.env.example` synchronized with required variables.
- Only `EXPO_PUBLIC_*` values may be exposed to the app bundle.
